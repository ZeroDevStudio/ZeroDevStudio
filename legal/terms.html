ZERO DEV STUDIO / LEGAL INFORMATION

Service terms.

A draft framework for project requests. It is not a final contract and must be reviewed by a parent/guardian and qualified adviser before use with customers.

DRAFT — do not rely on this as a final legal agreement. Fill in the legal service provider, business identity, delivery terms, revision limits, cancellation/refund rules, and dispute process before launch.

1. Project requests

A submitted ticket is an inquiry and does not itself create a contract, reserve a start date, or require the studio to begin work. The studio reviews the scope and may request clarification.

2. Quotations and scope

The studio provides a project-specific quotation based on the agreed pages, features, design requirements, content, integrations, timeline, and revision scope. Work should begin only after both parties accept the final written scope, price, timeline, and terms.

3. Prices and promotions

Displayed prices are starting examples, not guaranteed fixed packages. Currency conversion may change. The NEW26 promotion is intended for one eligible project per verified account, subject to the final offer terms and backend availability. The quotation should state whether a discount is included.

4. Payment method

GCash is currently the only payment method described by the studio. PayPal is not available yet. Payment details should be provided only through an official, verified communication channel after the quotation is accepted. Never share a GCash MPIN, OTP, PIN, password, or bank credential. Payment processing is not performed by this website.

5. Client content

Clients should submit only content, logos, images, and files they own or are permitted to use. The final terms should specify who owns delivered website code, designs, assets, domains, hosting accounts, and third-party licences.

6. Communication and account security

Clients are responsible for keeping account credentials private. Studio staff should never ask for account passwords, one-time codes, payment PINs, or other authentication secrets. Internal staff notes are for authorized staff and are not client messages.

7. Availability and limitations

Ticket submission and status information depend on service availability and configuration. The website does not guarantee a project acceptance, delivery date, specific business result, or uninterrupted availability.

8. Final agreement

A separate project agreement should cover deliverables, milestones, payment schedule, revisions, cancellation/refunds, maintenance, hosting, intellectual property, and dispute handling. Obtain guardian and legal review before accepting real clients.
