ZERO DEV STUDIO / LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

This is a draft template for review. It must be completed and approved by the adult project owner before live registration or real client data is enabled.

DRAFT — not a final legal notice. Confirm the business identity, contact address, data retention period, service providers, and applicable Philippine privacy requirements before launch.

1. Information the service may collect

When live features are enabled, ZeroDev Studio may process account name and email address, authentication records, project request details, budget ranges, messages, uploaded project files, ticket activity, and notification preferences.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure client accounts, review project requests, prepare quotations, communicate about a project, provide account support, and maintain service security and records.

3. Who can see project information

Clients can access their own tickets, client-visible messages, attachments, and notifications. Authorized staff accounts may access tickets to review and deliver the requested service. Staff-only notes are not displayed to clients. Access is intended to be enforced by database row-level security.

4. Service providers and storage

The planned backend uses Supabase for authentication, database, and private file storage. Email delivery may use a separately configured SMTP provider. The adult project owner must identify the actual providers and hosting locations before this notice is made effective.

5. Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, GCash MPINs, bank credentials, one-time passwords, government identification numbers, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through project forms or messages.

6. Retention and account requests

The adult project owner must set and publish retention periods, a contact channel for access/correction/deletion requests, and a procedure for security incidents before production launch. Do not assume that closing a ticket automatically deletes all records.

7. Children and guardian involvement

The service owner is a minor and must have a parent or guardian supervise business operations, customer agreements, accounts, and handling of personal information. The final service eligibility and guardian consent rules must be reviewed before launch.

8. Contact

Insert a monitored official support email and responsible business/contact identity here before enabling live registration.
