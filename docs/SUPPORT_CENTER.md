# ZeroDev Studio v0.9 — Support Center

Added a branded support experience at `support/index.html`.

## Visual direction

The support center uses an original ZeroDev Studio identity: navy support header, custom ZD mark, abstract blue/teal banner, help-topic cards, expandable articles, and a branded support-desk profile. It follows familiar help-center navigation patterns without copying Steam or Valve logos, wording, or visual assets.

## Support identity

**ZeroDev Support** is the official help-desk identity for this prototype. Its stated scope is account access, email verification, project requests, and service questions. It is not presented as a named individual or as a live human agent.

The interface clearly labels itself as a preview. Live ticket handling, staffed replies, and support response-time promises are not active yet.

## Included interactions

- Topic search filters article content.
- Popular-topic shortcuts populate the search field.
- Help categories link to their article groups.
- Shared System/Light/Dark appearance preference is supported.
- Account access and public website links are provided.

Do not publish fake online-status indicators, response-time guarantees, human-agent identities, or testimonials.
