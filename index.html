GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few details to make the process clearer before a project begins.

Is ₱1,275 the final price for every website?+

No. It is an approximate PHP equivalent of the $20 USD starting price in the approved specification. The final quotation depends on your project's pages, features, design, and overall scope.

What payment methods are available?+

GCash is the only currently accepted method. PayPal is coming soon but is not available yet. Official payment instructions should only be provided after a project quotation is reviewed and accepted.

Do I need to pay before receiving a quotation?+

No payment should be made just to request a quotation. The project scope, price, and terms should be discussed and agreed first.

Can I request a website within my budget?+

Yes. You can share a budget range. The studio will review whether the requested work can fit that range and explain any adjustments needed. A requested budget does not automatically determine the final price.

Can I submit a project online right now?+

The secure project ticket and client portal are still being developed. This version is a website prototype; it does not collect project submissions or personal information.
